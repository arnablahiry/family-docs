Lararium

A private wallet for your family's documents: passports, IDs, cards, certificates and logins.

Everything stays yours. Documents are encrypted on your phone. Backups go to your own Google Drive, encrypted, in a folder only this app can use. There are no ads, no tracking and no accounts on our side.
Groups. Share a group (your family, flatmates…) with other people's phones: each phone shares an encrypted copy from its own Drive, readable only with the group's code.
Get it. Lararium is an Android app, released on GitHub.
